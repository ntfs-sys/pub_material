![[p01hw 2.png]]Дано: $m=0,001кг, r_{12} = 1м, e_q = 1,602*10^{-19}Кл, \mu = 63,546 г/моль, z = 29$ Найти $F$
$$\nu = \frac{m}{M} = \frac{10^{-3}}{63,546*10^{-3}} = \frac{1}{63,546}моль = 1,5736*10^{-2} моль \\
N = \nu N_A = 1,5736*10^{-2}*6,022*10^{23} = 9,475*10^{21} \\
N_P = Nz = 9,475*10^{21}*29 = 2,748*10^{23} \\
Q_P = N_P e = 2,748*10^{23} * 1,602 * 10^{-19} = 4,402*10^4 Кл \\
Q = 0,01 * Q_P = 0,01*4,402*10^4 = 44,02 Кл \\
F = \frac{Q^2}{4\pi \epsilon_0 r^2} = \frac{44,02^2}{4\pi\cdot8,85418787\cdot10^{-12}\cdot1^2} = 1,74*10^{13}Н
$$
Ответ: $1,74*10^{13}Н$

Дано: $r = 0,1 м , q = 50 * 10^{-6} Кл, q_0 = 7*10^{-6} Кл$
![[p01hw 5 fig1.png]]
$$\lambda = \frac q{2\pi r} \\
\begin{cases}
dF_0 = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{q_0dq}{r^2} \\
dq = \lambda dl
\end{cases} \rightarrow dF_0 = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{q_0\lambda\,dl}{r^2} \\
F_0 = \frac{dF_0}{dl} = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \frac{q_0\lambda}{r^2} \\
\Delta T = f_0 r = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \frac{q_0\lambda}{r} = \frac{q_0q}{8\pi^2 \epsilon_0r^2} = 50H
$$
Ответ 50Н

![[p01hw 28.png]]
Дано: $R, q, -q, l, R=0,3м, l = 0,52 м, q = 0,4 *10^{-6}кл$ Найти: $\Delta \phi$
![[p01hw fig1.png]]
$$
\phi_1 = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}(\frac qR - \frac q {\sqrt{R^2 + l^2}}) \\
\phi_2 = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}(\frac q {\sqrt{R^2 + l^2}} - \frac qR )\\
\Delta \phi = \phi_1 - \phi_2 = \frac{2q}{4\pi \epsilon_0}(\frac{1}{R} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{R^2+l^2}}) = 1,2*10^4 В = 12 кВ
$$
Ответ: 12кВ
